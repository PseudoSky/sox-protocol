## Overview

The Open Invention Network (OIN) is the largest patent non-aggression community in history. Joining commits the publisher to non-aggression on Linux-system patents in exchange for reciprocal coverage from ~4000 members including Google, IBM, Microsoft, and Amazon. Membership is free for organizations under $10M revenue.

Joining OIN is a recommended step in the SOX Protocol IP strategy. See the patent landscape analysis for full rationale.

## Why OIN

From the patent landscape analysis:

> OIN membership (free under $10M revenue under OIN 2.0). Joining commits the publisher to non-aggression on Linux-system patents in exchange for reciprocal coverage from ~4000 members including Google, IBM, Microsoft, Amazon. Low cost, meaningful protection, signal value.

The SOX Protocol's agent-communication primitives are deeply rooted in prior art (KQML 1993, FIPA-ACL 2000, JADE 1999–). OIN membership adds an institutional layer of protection and signals to the open-source community that SOX Protocol is committed to a patent-safe ecosystem.
